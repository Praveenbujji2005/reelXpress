Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding how our creators shoot, edit, and deliver fast on site.

What exactly is reelXpress? expand_more
reelXpress is India's premier platform where you can instantly book a certified Reel Maker who arrives at your event, captures high-caliber vertical footage, edits it on the spot, and delivers the finished reel before the moment wraps up. No waiting weeks, no follow-ups, no stress.
How is reelXpress different from a regular videographer? expand_more
Your traditional photographer captures high-res portraits, and your main videographer tells the long-form story after months of editing. reelXpress delivers the content that goes live on Instagram while your event is actually happening. We are the missing piece for viral social presence.
Can I book last minute? Like, the day before? expand_more
Yes — clients do this regularly! As long as a verified creator is available in your city zone, we can lock down your slot within minutes. We have successfully arranged same-day shoots multiple times across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Vizag, and Vijayawada.
Can I see my Reel Maker's portfolio before the shoot? expand_more
Absolutely. We happily share creator profiles and sample reels upon request when booking, ensuring their specific aesthetic, camera movement, and pacing align seamlessly with your vision.
