## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an uncompromising, high-end cinematic aesthetic tailored for premier short-form video creators and modern digital narrative agencies. It merges dramatic film-studio lighting with the crisp precision of contemporary high-craft editorial design. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Dark Cinematic Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian foundations textured with rich, smoky amber-brown undertones.
- Luminescent, focused amber-orange lighting that mimics 35mm tungsten film glow and anamorphic lens light falloff.
- Multi-layered frosted glass plates with hairline specular borders (`1px` translucent strokes) that lend physical presence to floating digital cards.
- Hyper-polished typography drawing inspiration from modern Swiss editorial typefaces, presenting an aura of luxury, mastery, and viral storytelling precision.

## Colors

The palette balances profound darkness with intense, warm luminosity:

- **Primary (`#FF6D29`)**: Radiates cinematic tungsten light. Reserved for primary interactive accents, focus states, ambient glow halos, key conversions, and signature metrics.
- **Secondary (`#453027`)**: An espresso-amber shade creating cinematic depth transitions, vignette backdrops, and gradient fills that break the starkness of pure black.
- **Tertiary (`#BABABA`)**: A refined, low-reflection cool grey dedicated to secondary typography, video metadata, and muted graphic accents.
- **Neutral (`#161316`)**: The obsidian canvas tone, providing near-black baseline contrast without absolute zero-light desaturation.
- **Specular & Glow Tokens**: Semi-transparent overlays create luminous edges and atmospheric radial bleeds behind focal vertical video canvases.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** throughout, creating an editorial feel through deliberate weight distribution and tight negative tracking on display scales.

- **Hero & Large Headlines**: Set with aggressive negative tracking (`-0.04em` to `-0.02em`) and tight line-heights to emulate cinema billing and editorial magazines.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for maximum legibility on deep dark backdrops, using slightly increased leading and neutral tertiary contrast (`#BABABA`) to prevent visual eye strain.
- **Labels & Overlays**: Capitalized small weights with expanded tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) for metric callouts, video aspect tags (`9:16`), and live engagement stats.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a fluid 12-column grid system bounded inside a maximum content width of `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous canvas margins (`4rem`). Ample negative space allows glass surfaces and ambient orange glows to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Interactive video showcases reflow into asymmetric pairs or carousels.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Section margins compress to `space-2xl` while keeping card touch points comfortable.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: All margins and component paddings strictly follow the 8pt core scale, using `space-xs` through `space-3xl` tokens to preserve compositional cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built via layered glassmorphism, multi-stop radial spotlights, and calibrated specular halos rather than stark drop shadows:

1. **Base Ground (Level 0)**: Obsidian surface (`#161316`) overlaid with soft, dynamic linear-to-radial gradients blending towards dark coffee brown (`#453027`).
2. **Glass Planes (Level 1)**: Semi-transparent backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px); background: rgba(22, 19, 22, 0.65)`) framed by a subtle specular top-to-bottom stroke (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
3. **Elevated Stage & Modal Surfaces (Level 2)**: Elevated cards utilize a deeper blur (`32px`), a warm amber border edge (`rgba(255, 109, 41, 0.35)`), and an ambient soft shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`).
4. **Cinematic Ambient Illumination**: Prominent showcase pieces (such as 9:16 vertical video cards) feature an ambient backlight glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 80px rgba(255, 109, 41, 0.22)`), giving the appearance of light cast directly from the video onto dark studio walls.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary to echo contemporary high-end smartphone hardware and modern cinema equipment:

- **Default UI Elements & Buttons**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius for subtle, balanced curvature.
- **Content Cards & Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius to provide sleek, organic framing for preview modules and nested items.
- **Vertical Video Canvases & Feature Hubs (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (`24px`) corner radius, deliberately mimicking an iPhone display silhouette.
- **Pill Badges & Engagement Tags**: Full border radius (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical tags, metrics, and filter buttons from rectangular structural frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glow CTA**: High-contrast `#FF6D29` solid fill with black or stark white bold text (`#FFFFFF`). Accompanied by an orange shadow glow (`0 0 24px rgba(255, 109, 41, 0.45)`) that expands on hover. Transitions scale smoothly to `1.02`.
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Semi-translucent base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and crisp white text. On hover, shifts to an orange-tinted rim (`rgba(255, 109, 41, 0.5)`).

### 9:16 Vertical Video Showcase Cards
- Explicit aspect ratio of `9:16` engineered for mobile video playback and Instagram Reels presentation.
- Features `rounded-xl` corners with smooth overflow clipping.
- Glassmorphic top metadata bar with micro Instagram badge, view count chip, and agency watermark.
- Subtle inner gradient overlay at the foot of each card to guarantee text readability against varying video footage.

### Glassmorphic Pricing & Feature Cards
- Dark frosted container (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px); background: rgba(22, 19, 22, 0.7)`).
- Featured plan includes an active gradient border stroke transitioning from `#FF6D29` to `#453027`, complete with a "Most Popular" floating pill badge.
- Inner list dividers set with hairline opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- Fully radiused pill shapes with dark tint fills (`rgba(255, 109, 41, 0.12)`) and contrasting typography (`#FF6D29`).
- Used for metrics (e.g., "+3.4M Views", "Viral Format", "Edited in 4K").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Dark obsidian base (`#0E0C0E`), inset border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), and padded placeholder styling in muted grey (`#BABABA`).
- Focus state brings an orange rim highlight and subtle surrounding focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 109, 41, 0.2)`).
